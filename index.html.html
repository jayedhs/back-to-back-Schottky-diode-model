Enter or review every parameter before calculating.

SBH equations

I0 = AcA* T² exp[−ΦB/Vth]
ΦB = Vth ln(AcA*T²/I0)   [eV]
1/E* = (1−νtip²)/Etip + (1−νflake²)/Eflake
a = [3FR/(4E*)]1/3,   d = a²/R,   Ac = π(a²+d²)

A* = 1.20173 × 10⁶(m*/m₀) A m⁻² K⁻² when calculated from effective mass. The extracted ΦB is model-dependent and should not be treated as an intrinsic barrier without temperature-dependent validation.
